- **Then why did Dimensity 7300 drop from 60 FPS to 30–47 FPS, and why didn't Redmi 10A turn its new 26% free `VUThread` headroom (`74.0%`) into higher FPS?**
  Because of **3 Pacing / Synchronization changes in Steps 3, 4, & 5** that forced `EEThread` and `GSThread` to sleep on mutexes and skip frames:
  1. **`PS2X_MTVU_VBLANK_WAIT=1` + `waitPipelineIdle()` in `EeScheduler.cpp` (`hostCatchUp`, `checkpointDue`, and `processDueDeadlines`)**:
     - Look at what `ps2x::asyncvif::waitPipelineIdle()` does in `ps2_async_vif.cpp`: it locks `worker->mutex` and `worker->gif.mutex` and sleeps on `std::condition_variable::wait_until` until **both `VUThread` and `GIFThread` are 100% empty and idle**!
     - Calling `waitPipelineIdle()` from `EEThread` **serialized `EEThread` behind `VUThread` and `GIFThread`**—destroying Multi-Threaded VU1 (`MTVU`) parallelism!
     - On the **Dimensity 7300**, serializing `EEThread` behind `waitPipelineIdle()` added 4–12 ms of mutex/condvar sleep stalls per frame, dropping framerate from **60 FPS locked down to 30–47 FPS**!
     - On the **Redmi 10A**, serializing `EEThread` behind `waitPipelineIdle()` put `EEThread` to sleep while `VUThread` worked (and then left `VUThread` idle while `EEThread` worked), which is why **both `EEThread` (`82.7%`) and `VUThread` (`74.0%`) sat below 85% CPU** instead of running at 100% parallel throughput to boost FPS!
  2. **`PS2X_MTVU_STREAM=1` in `main.cpp`**:
     - Forced `VUThread` to lock `worker->gif.mutex` and wake `GIFThread` every **32 KB** (~105 mutex locks + condition-variable wakeups per combat frame on `3.28 MB` of GIF data!) instead of batching cleanly at `256–512 KB`.
  3. **`options.SkipDuplicateFrames = true;` in `Bridge.cpp`**:
     - Caused PCSX2's `GSRendererHW` to skip presenting every 2nd VSync on the Dimensity 7300, causing severe 30–47 FPS frame-pacing judder.
