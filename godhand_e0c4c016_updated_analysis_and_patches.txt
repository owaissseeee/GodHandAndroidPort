--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

COP1 FPU + COP2 (VU0 Macro Mode) instructions in 1 cycle directly in registers.
In the static recompilation (`gh_unity_batch_00..63.cpp`), every single MIPS
instruction expands into C++ macros from `ps2_runtime_macros.h`. Right now,
those macros are forcing the in-order Cortex-A53 core on `EEThread` to execute
15 to 35 instructions for every 1 PS2 instruction:

CULPRIT #1: `ps2FpuAdd` and `ps2FpuSub` Software Mantissa Alignment (`ps2_runtime_macros.h:164-193`)
   - Every single guest `ADD.S` (`FPU_ADD_S`) and `SUB.S` (`FPU_SUB_S`) calls
     `ps2FpuAlignOperands(a, b)` + `ps2FpuClamp(a +/- b)`.
   - `ps2FpuAlignOperands` copies both floats `a` and `b` from FPU registers
     into integer registers (`fmov w0, s0` = 5-cycle stall on Cortex-A53),
     extracts their exponents, runs a 4-branch `if / else if` tree with variable
     bit-shifts, copies them back to FPU registers (`fmov s0, w0` = 5-cycle stall),
     adds them, and then `ps2FpuClamp` copies the result BACK to an integer
     register again!
   - That turns a 1-cycle ARM64 `fadd s0, s0, s1` into ~28 instructions and 5
     branches on every single float add/subtract in physics, animations, bones,
     and AI!
   - FIX: On ARM64, `FPCR.FZ` (Flush-to-Zero) and `FPCR.DN` (Default NaN) are
     already enabled in hardware at boot. Replacing `ps2FpuAdd`, `ps2FpuSub`,
     and `FPU_MUL_S` with direct hardware `a + b`, `a - b`, `a * b` (or

CULPRIT #2: `FPU_C_EQ_S`, `FPU_C_OLT_S`, `FPU_C_OLE_S` Software Float Comparisons (`ps2_runtime_macros.h:210-216, 912-925`)

{
#if defined(__aarch64__) || defined(_M_ARM64)

    const int32_t diff = static_cast<int32_t>((ua >> 23) & 0xFFu) - static_cast<int32_t>((ub >> 23) & 0xFFu);
